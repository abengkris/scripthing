# Initial Concept
Scripthing is a local-first web-based screenplay writing application, allowing users to write scripts with industry-standard formatting. It features optional AI-assisted writing that users can configure with their own keys.

## Product Vision
To provide a distraction-free, professional-grade screenplay writing experience that empowers writers with local-first data ownership and optional, privacy-focused AI capabilities.

## Goals
- Professional-grade formatting (Scene Heading, Action, Character, Dialogue, etc.).
- Local-first architecture using a SQLite database.
- Seamless AI integration (Chat, Suggest, Rewrite) for an optional writing boost.
- High-quality exports (PDF, Final Draft .fdx).

## Target Audience
- Screenwriters, playwrights, and podcast creators who prioritize privacy and local data control.
